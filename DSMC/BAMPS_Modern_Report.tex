\section{Executive Summary}
This report details the completion and validation of the modern BAMPS GPU engine. The architecture has been refactored into a high-performance, template-based policy framework supporting Classical, Relativistic, and Ultrarelativistic regimes. Key physical corrections, including Vlasov-corrected Lennard-Jones potentials and dual-path pressure validation (Virial vs. Wall), have been successfully implemented and verified.

\section{Architecture Design}
The engine utilizes a C++ template-based policy architecture to eliminate macro-dependency and ensure type-safe physical combinations:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Kinematics}: \texttt{Classical}, \texttt{Relativistic}, \texttt{Ultrarelativistic} (massless limit).
    \item \textbf{Thermostats}: \texttt{Andersen}, \texttt{Langevin}, and a generic \texttt{Nosé-Hoover Chain (NHC)} supporting all kinematics via generic kinetic energy reduction.
    \item \textbf{Potentials}: \texttt{LennardJones} with force capping, analytical tail corrections ($P_{tail}$), and mean-field Vlasov attraction $a(T)$.
    \item \textbf{Collisions}: Integrated \texttt{HardSphereCollision} (DSMC) for hybrid MD+DSMC simulations.
\end{itemize}

\section{Physical Validation: Thermostats and Canonical Ensembles}
Initial testing revealed a ``numerical heat explosion'' in random initialization at high densities ($\rho=6.0$). This was resolved by implementing a \textbf{Simple Cubic Lattice} initialization. 

The performance of different thermostats was compared by tracking the system's relaxation to the target temperature $T=1.2$. As shown in Figure~\ref{fig:therm_comp}, all active thermostats successfully stabilized the system and produced momentum distributions matching the theoretical Maxwell-Boltzmann PDF. NHC provided the most deterministic and smooth control, while Andersen and Langevin showed characteristic fluctuations consistent with their stochastic nature.

\section{Equation of State (EoS) Comprehensive Validation}
To rigorously verify the physical fidelity of the engine, a 4D parameter scan was conducted across $\sigma$, $\epsilon$, $T$, and $\rho$. The results, presented in Figure~\ref{fig:eos_scan}, focus on the $P_{wall}/T$ metric (equivalent to $\rho Z$) to ensure robustness against virial noise.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Sigma Scan}: Captures the non-linear growth of excluded volume effects, accurately tracking the Carnahan-Starling prediction as $\sigma$ increases.
    \item \textbf{Epsilon Scan}: Demonstrates the linear reduction in pressure due to mean-field Vlasov attraction.
    \item \textbf{Temperature Scan}: Shows the relative weakening of attractive forces as kinetic energy increases, with $P/T$ asymptotically approaching the hard-sphere limit.
    \item \textbf{Density Scan}: Validates the engine's stability and accuracy from dilute gas ($\rho=1.0$) to high-density liquid ($\rho=8.0$) regimes.
\end{itemize}
