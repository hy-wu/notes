\section{Executive Summary}
This report details the completion and validation of the modern BAMPS GPU engine. The architecture utilizes a high-performance, template-based policy framework supporting Classical, Relativistic, and Ultrarelativistic regimes. We have established rigorous physical credibility by benchmarking against standard Lennard-Jones (LJ) fluids and Argon structures.

\section{Architecture Design}
The engine utilizes a C++ template-based policy architecture. A key feature is the support for both local-acting and global-reduction thermostats:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Thermostats}: Includes \texttt{Andersen}, \texttt{Langevin}, and a true \texttt{Nosé-Hoover Chain (NHC)}.
    \item \textbf{NHC Implementation}: Unlike local thermostats, NHC is implemented as a global operator. It performs a GPU kinetic energy reduction, propagates the 3-node chain state on the Host (MTK algorithm), and applies a uniform momentum scaling factor to the GPU particle array. This ensures rigorous canonical ensemble sampling.
    \item \textbf{Potentials}: \texttt{LennardJones} with $2.5\sigma$ cutoff and force capping. Current validation is performed in pure MD mode (\texttt{NullCollision}) to isolate potential effects from DSMC stochasticity.
\end{itemize}

\section{Physical Validation: MD Credibility Suite}
To verify the structural and dynamical integrity of the engine, we performed a benchmark on liquid Argon ($\rho^*=0.8, T^*=1.0$). 
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Structure}: The Radial Distribution Function $g(r)$ (Figure~\ref{fig:argon}) exhibits the characteristic peaks of a classical fluid, with the first peak correctly positioned at $r \approx 1.12\sigma$.
    \item \textbf{Conservation}: NVE simulations demonstrate negligible energy drift over 15,000 steps, confirming the stability of the Velocity Verlet integrator.
\end{itemize}

\section{Performance and Stability Diagnostics}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Thermal Stability}: Figure~\ref{fig:temp_stab} confirms that all thermostats maintain the target temperature within 1\% error across the entire scan range.
    \item \textbf{Scalability}: Performance scaling (ms/step) tracks linearly with the complexity of the neighborhood search as $\sigma$ or $\rho$ increases.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}
The BAMPS GPU engine is physically robust and numerically stable. The successful reproduction of Argon structures and LJ EoS using NHC thermostats provides a solid foundation for future relativistic QGP simulations.
